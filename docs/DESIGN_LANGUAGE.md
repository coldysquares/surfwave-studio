# Graphic Web Utility → SURFwave

SURFwave inherits the shared design doctrine:

> **PERSONALITY IN THE SHELL.**  
> **CLARITY IN THE TOOL.**

This is a principle, not a shared skin. SURFwave should never become a WOLLOHY page with audio controls pasted into it.

## SURFwave-specific translation

### Shell

The product may be expressive through:

- the surfboard/fin + wave mark
- coastal motion and signal motifs
- strong room identity
- large confident labels at orientation moments
- sharp composition and visible divisions

### Work areas

The deeper a user enters Studio or Voice Lab, the calmer the UI becomes.

- Home / Projects: expressive
- Room navigation: characterful but obvious
- Studio workspace: restrained
- Mixer / recording / performance: highly functional
- Focus / listening / stage-like states: minimal

## Product vocabulary

Brand motifs should come from audio work rather than generic decoration:

- waveforms
- transport marks
- meter ticks
- stems / lanes
- takes
- signal flow
- cue markers
- recording-state indicators
- subtle surf/wave movement where it does not compete with controls

Avoid random blobs, gratuitous gradients, glass panels, pill-heavy controls, and generic producer-dashboard aesthetics.

## Typography

The SURFwave wordmark is custom artwork and remains the headliner. `Studio`, `Voice Lab`, and other room descriptors stay subordinate.

UI typography should prioritize legibility. A display face may carry orientation moments, but actual editing, mixing, diagnostics, and forms must remain exceptionally readable.

## Color

SURFwave keeps its own palette rather than inheriting WOLLOHY or TabbyCat signal colors:

- Deep Shore `#403742`
- Coastal Blue `#89B5CC`
- Mist `#E2DCE8`
- Ocean Depth `#56548C`
- Twilight `#6D6380`

Deep Shore + Coastal Blue + Mist should do most of the work. Secondary purples support structure rather than turning the interface into a purple haze.

## Components

Favor clear hard divisions, compact controls, intentional borders, strong grouping, and flat confident surfaces.

Do not turn every value into a rounded card. Do not use novelty styling inside high-attention controls such as meters, transport, mixer strips, record states, exports, or training progress.

## Interaction

Motion should explain state or reinforce tactility. It should be short and decisive.

Stillness is valuable during listening, recording, mixing, model training, and export.

## Copy

Functional copy stays functional:

`IMPORT`, `SEPARATE`, `RECORD`, `MIX`, `EXPORT`, `TRAIN MODEL`, `USE MODEL`, `SAVE PROJECT`.

Dry humor can live in noncritical empty states or diagnostics. Do not make users decode cute wording to operate audio software.

## Success test

SURFwave should feel:

- unusual when opened
- obvious within seconds
- fast once working
- recognizable without its logo

The app should feel intentionally authored, but never make the user pay an usability tax for the branding.
